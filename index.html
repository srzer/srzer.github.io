About me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the University of Washington. Previously, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science (Yao class), with a minor in Literature, from Tsinghua University. I'm currently working on principled algorithm design and theoretical analysis of deep learning and foundation models. Recently, I'm also interested in combinatorics, and the application of AI in medicine.
